Location & the surrounding area

179 Old Highway 17 sits right in Plantagenet — on Old Highway 17 across from the gas station, just off County Road 17 — a village in the Township of Alfred and Plantagenet, in the United Counties of Prescott and Russell — a Franco-Ontarian township of 9,949 people (2021 Census) where most residents are bilingual, set between the Ottawa and South Nation rivers.

Between two big marketsThe township's own investment guide puts downtown Ottawa about 45 minutes west and Montreal about 80 minutes east, with Highway 417 roughly a half-hour away — your customers and staff can come from both directions along County Road 17.
A real village around youThe township office is just up the street at 205 Old Highway 17, and the Serge R. Lalonde Community Centre is on Main Street. Plantagenet has its own French-language high school (École secondaire catholique de Plantagenet, grades 7–12, on County Road 17) fed by local elementary schools, including Saint-Paul in the village — a genuine family community, not a drive-through strip.
Health services clusterPrescott-Russell Paramedic Services has a station in Plantagenet, and the bilingual Hawkesbury & District General Hospital — the county's hospital — serves the whole region from Hawkesbury, about 30 km east. For a health, care or wellness tenant, you're in the middle of the local care network.
Your catchmentBeyond the village (1,056 residents, 2021), the building draws from the whole township (9,949) and the Prescott-Russell corridor communities along County Road 17 — Alfred, Wendover, Rockland to the west, L'Orignal and Hawkesbury to the east.

Common questions

When is the space available?

January 1, 2027. The current medical clinic's lease ends December 31, 2026.

What condition will the space be in?

The landlord returns the space to base condition at the landlord's cost. You then complete your own fit-up, designed for your business.

How much is the rent?

The published base rent is $21.50 per sq. ft. + HST on a triple-net basis (MLS® X14122876) — about $86,108 per year (≈ $7,176/month) in base rent for the 4,005 sq. ft., plus operating costs and taxes (annual property taxes are published on the listing at $20,246.40). Contact Chantal to discuss your terms.

Is the pharmacy part of what's for lease?

No. The pharmacy occupies the other portion of the building under its own lease (to May 28, 2028). It stays — and keeps bringing daily visitors to the building.

Can I use the space for my type of business?

Possibly — daycare, professional office, medical/health and service businesses are all good fits. Every use is subject to permitted uses and zoning, so confirm with the Township of Alfred and Plantagenet early in the conversation.
